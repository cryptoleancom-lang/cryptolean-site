Chart description

val-v1 · Two heuristic levels, not a model: the 730-day average and that average times 5. The only claim this chart supports is how much time price has actually spent outside each, which is stated below it. Both lines need two years of record before they exist at all, so the early history is blank rather than extrapolated.
