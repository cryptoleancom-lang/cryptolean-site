Description

val-v1 · The multiples 1.6×, 2×, 3×, 5×, 8×, 13×, 21× are the published Fibonacci-derived constants, repeated as published and NOT refitted — refitting a folklore level and calling the result a finding is worse than repeating it with the provenance attached. There is no mechanism behind them. The percentile beside them is the honest counterpart: where today's multiple sits in this asset's own expanding record, computed without any data from after each date.
