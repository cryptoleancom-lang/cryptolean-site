Description

A bottom is only identifiable after the recovery has happened; this is a hindsight comparison, not a timing signal. Anchors use price alternation after a 60% fall, not the risk metric. Every chart here assumes cycles continue in roughly the shape they have so far. Nothing in the data can tell you when that stops being true.
