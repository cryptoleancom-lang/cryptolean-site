Chart description

val-v1 · logreg-v2 · Bands are expanding quantiles of the residual, so the fan is allowed to be lopsided and it is: the upside band sits further from the fit than the downside one. Price has spent most of its record ABOVE the least-squares line — an expanding fit lags a rising trend by construction, and that is a property of the method rather than a valuation signal.
