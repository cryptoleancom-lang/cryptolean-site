Description

cycle-v5 · Measured against the RUNNING all-time high, so no future peak is used and the counter is computable on the day. A counter resets to zero the moment the fall happens and then climbs, so a tall line means a long calm stretch — not that one is overdue. Nothing here implies a schedule.
