Chart description

A bottom is only identifiable after the recovery has happened; this is a hindsight comparison, not a timing signal. Anchors use price alternation after a 60% fall, not the risk metric. Every chart here assumes cycles continue in roughly the shape they have so far. Nothing in the data can tell you when that stops being true.

How to read it

Every value plotted for a past date could have been computed on that date. Normalised series expand their window rather than scaling against the full history, so nothing here is drawn using information that did not exist yet — which is also why the early years of a normalised series are dropped rather than shown against a range of one.
