Chart description

DefiLlama /summary/fees/{chain} totalDataChart · dataType=dailyRevenue · source-native signed daily USD revenue under each chain adapter's accounting. Ethereum reports burned base and blob fees: its zero history before EIP-1559 is real economics, not missing coverage. Base is published as burned coins and includes negative source observations; they remain negative and are not clipped. Bars are green above zero, red below zero and neutral at zero. Logarithmic and line/area controls are intentionally absent because they would hide signed values or discard the declared sign encoding. There is no All option because no independent public all-chain history is published. Chain Revenue is distinct from Chain Fees, Protocol Fees, Protocol Revenue, Holders Revenue and REV, and is not real-time.
