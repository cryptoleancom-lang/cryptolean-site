Description

tech-v5 · Computed on LOG price. On a series that has run five orders of magnitude, a raw-price histogram is dominated by the level: a $400 move in 2026 and a $4 move in 2013 are the same proportional event and the raw version says one is a hundred times larger. 12/26/9, at their conventional values.

Usage

MACD -0.0002 · signal +0.0007 · histogram -0.0009

Every value plotted for a past date could have been computed on that date. Normalised series expand their window rather than scaling against the full history, so nothing here is drawn using information that did not exist yet — which is also why the early years of a normalised series are dropped rather than shown against a range of one.
