Description

cycle-v5 · Plotted on the date the period ENDED, so a point in 2026 is the return of someone who bought four years earlier. Overlapping windows are not independent observations: fifteen years contains about three non-overlapping four-year holds, and the percentages below should be read with that in mind rather than as a probability.

Usage

1y positive on 54% of start dates · 2y positive on 51% of start dates · 4y positive on 42% of start dates

Every value plotted for a past date could have been computed on that date. Normalised series expand their window rather than scaling against the full history, so nothing here is drawn using information that did not exist yet — which is also why the early years of a normalised series are dropped rather than shown against a range of one.
