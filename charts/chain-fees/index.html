Chart description

DefiLlama /summary/fees/{chain} totalDataChart · dataType=dailyFees · source-native daily USD gas or transaction fees paid by users of the selected chain. Ethereum includes base, priority and blob fees; Base uses DefiLlama's chain-adapter gas-fee definition. Ethereum and Base are fetched independently, all source zeros remain zero and absent dates remain null. There is no All option because no independent public all-chain history is published; Cryptolean never fabricates it by summing a partial chain set. Chain Fees are distinct from Protocol Fees, Protocol Revenue, Holders Revenue, Chain Revenue and REV, and are not real-time.
